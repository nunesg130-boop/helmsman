# Community code of conduct

Helmsman welcomes constructive participation from people of every background
and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Respect privacy and never pressure someone to expose their infrastructure,
  identity, or credentials for troubleshooting.
- Help keep issues and reviews focused and accessible.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, credential exposure, sustained disruption, and personal
attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when conduct is unsafe or persistently disruptive.
Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and prior behavior.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/nunesg130-boop/helmsman/security/advisories/new),
begin the report title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`, and include only information
needed to investigate. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
